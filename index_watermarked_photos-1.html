About me

Chasing faint light.

I'm Jonathan, an astrophotographer based in Norfolk with a fascination for the night sky and the incredible objects beyond it.

My interest in astronomy grew into a passion for capturing my own images of galaxies, nebulae, planets and the Moon. What started with simply looking up at the stars has become a hobby involving telescopes, cameras, tracking mounts and many long nights under the Norfolk sky.

I enjoy the technical side as much as the photography. Each image is built from many exposures, carefully stacked and processed to reveal details invisible to the naked eye.

The Norfolk Nebula is my personal collection of those images, documenting my astrophotography journey one target at a time.
